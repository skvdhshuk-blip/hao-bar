HaoBar 隐私政策 HaoBar Privacy Policy

HaoBar 是运行在你 Mac 上的菜单栏工具。 HaoBar is a menu bar utility that runs on your Mac.

我们收集什么 What we collect

什么都不收集。HaoBar 不创建账号，不上传菜单栏内容、图标名、文件或标识符，也不向服务器发送使用事件。 Nothing. HaoBar does not create an account, does not upload menu bar contents, icon names, files, or identifiers, and does not send usage events to a server.

什么留在这台 Mac What stays on your Mac

设置、配置，以及可选的自定义菜单栏图标，都存在应用的沙箱容器里。 Settings, profiles, and an optional custom menu bar icon are stored in the app’s sandbox container.

权限 Permissions

  • 辅助功能 — 用来识别和整理菜单栏图标。HaoBar 不读其他应用的窗口，也不记录按键。 Accessibility — required to detect and arrange menu bar icons. HaoBar does not read other apps’ windows or log keystrokes.
  • 屏幕录制 — 只用来截菜单栏，好在弹条里显示隐藏图标。HaoBar 不录像，不截其他窗口，也不上传这些截图。截图时系统可能出现紫色「正在截屏」提示。 Screen Recording — used only to snapshot the menu bar so hidden icons can appear in the popup bar. HaoBar does not record video, does not capture other windows, and does not upload those snapshots. macOS may show a purple screen-capture indicator while a snapshot is taken.
  • Apple Events — 让快捷指令和 AppleScript 可以控制 HaoBar 自己。 Apple Events — so Shortcuts and AppleScript can control HaoBar itself.

更新 Updates

HaoBar 通过 Mac App Store 更新。没有 Sparkle，也没有第三方更新检查。 HaoBar updates through the Mac App Store. There is no Sparkle feed and no third-party update check.
